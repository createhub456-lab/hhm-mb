# AI Automation Agency — Shared Playbook (Agent Knowledge Base)

> This is the single source of truth all three agents share. It distills the
> operating playbook the agency runs on. All figures below come from the
> playbook itself — do not invent new numbers, results, or case studies.

## Core Premise

**"Market first, then offer, then pipeline, then message. The build comes fifth."**

Two business models:

- **The Consultant** — sells audits, strategy, documentation. First sale $500–2,000 (₹40k–₹1.5L). 1–2 weeks to cash.
- **The Builder** — delivers working automation systems. First sale $2,000–7,000. 3–6 weeks to cash.

Start as a **Consultant** even if you can build: audits sell faster, need no code, fund
operations, and reveal which builds clients will actually pay for.

## The Seven Steps

1. **Market** — Pick ONE narrow vertical (e.g. "multi-location dental clinics with 3–15 chairs", not "healthcare"). Validate: 200 real companies exist, 5 problem interviews done, the target buyer shows up consistently.
2. **Offer** — A paid discovery audit, 1–2 weeks, ₹40k–₹1.5L ($500–2,000). Includes interviews, process mapping, workflow analysis, a ranked list of automation opportunities + build roadmap. **Credit the audit fee against the build if they sign within 30 days.**
3. **Pipeline** — Outbound from week one: LinkedIn Sales Navigator + email enrichment + cold email. Target **100 contacts/week**. Run email on separate domains to protect deliverability.
4. **Message** — Talk about a specific *process*, not technology. Structure: (a) proof of research, (b) the manual task in their language, (c) quantified result, (d) small ask (a reply, not a meeting).
5. **Deliver** — One full workflow in two weeks, built in the client's own stack. Error handling, parallel testing with human verification, documented handover. No partial builds, no feature creep — explicit scope doc with a "not included" list.
6. **Retain** — Propose a retainer on the go-live call. Tiers ₹40k–₹5L/month: monitoring, monthly reports (hours + money saved), included improvement hours, one quarterly new build.
7. **Agent Team** — Automate your own back office with Claude agents: prospect research, proposal writing, audit synthesis, QA, report generation, content. **Agents draft, humans send — nothing reaches a client without human review.**

## Unit Economics (playbook figures)

- Conservative monthly pipeline: **400 LinkedIn contacts + 2,000 emails ≈ 6 calls booked → 2 audits + 1 build**.
- One build/month at ₹3L ≈ **₹36L annual run rate** before retainers.
- Pricing: **Build price = 20–35% of year-one value saved**; **Retainer = 10–15% of annual value**.
- Payments: **50% audit upfront, 50% build on go-live; retainers billed monthly in advance**.

## Critical Rules (non-negotiable)

1. Never let an agent write **unsupervised** to client systems in week one.
2. Strict scope boundaries — new requests become "phase two".
3. Block **two hours daily** for pipeline, even during heavy delivery.
4. Keep **no client above 40%** of revenue.
5. Turn outbound on in week one and **never fully stop it**.

## Timeline

- Days 1–5: Niche, 200-company list, domains, inbox warm-up.
- Days 6–14: Problem interviews, audit offer built, sequences prepared.
- Days 15–30: Campaigns live, first calls booked.
- Days 31–60: First audit sold, delivered, build proposal presented.
- Days 61–90: First build live, retainer proposed, second audit underway.

Solo until ~₹8L MRR, then hire (delivery engineers at 3+ builds, appointment
setters at 15+ calls/week, account managers at 8+ retainers).
