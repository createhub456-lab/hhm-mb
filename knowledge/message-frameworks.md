# Message Frameworks (Step 4)

> The playbook's message rule: **talk about a specific process, not technology.**
> Every message = 4 parts:
> 1. **Proof of research** — something specific about them (shows it's not a blast).
> 2. **The manual task in their language** — name the painful process plainly.
> 3. **Quantified result** — hours/money, but ONLY numbers you can honestly stand behind. If you have no proof yet, use a modest, clearly-hypothetical framing ("clinics your size usually...") — never invent a client result.
> 4. **Small ask** — a reply, not a meeting.

## Honesty guardrails for all copy
- Never invent a client, result, statistic, or logo.
- If you have no case study, say so implicitly by asking a question instead of claiming an outcome.
- Ranges and "typically/usually" are fine; fake specifics are not.

## Cold Email 1 (first touch)

**Subject options (short, lowercase, no hype):**
- `{{first_name}} — {{process}} question`
- `quick one about {{company}}'s {{process}}`

**Body:**
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Saw {{specific_research_detail}} — {{proof_it_is_relevant_to_them}}.

Most {{niche}} I talk to still handle {{manual_process}} by hand, which usually
eats {{time_estimate}} a week per {{unit}}.

We map that one process and show exactly where the time goes — no software pitch,
just the workflow.

Worth me sending over what that looks like for a place like {{company}}?

{{signature}}
```

## Cold Email 2 (follow-up, +3 days)
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Quick follow-up — the piece most {{niche}} miss on {{manual_process}} is
{{one_specific_insight}}.

Happy to point out where it's likely happening at {{company}}. Want me to?

{{signature}}
```

## Cold Email 3 (value drop, +4 days)
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Not chasing — just useful: here's the 3-step version of how {{niche}} usually
cut {{manual_process}} time:
1. {{step_1}}
2. {{step_2}}
3. {{step_3}}

If it's helpful I'll tailor it to {{company}}. Reply "send it" and I will.

{{signature}}
```

## Cold Email 4 (breakup, +5 days)
```
Hi {{first_name}},

I'll stop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If {{manual_process}} ever
becomes worth an hour of looking at, just reply and I'll pick it back up.

{{signature}}
```

## LinkedIn connection note (<300 chars)
```
Hi {{first_name}} — work with {{niche}} on {{manual_process}}. Not selling,
just comparing notes on how places your size handle it. Open to connecting?
```

## LinkedIn follow-up (after accept)
```
Thanks for connecting, {{first_name}}. Genuinely curious — how does {{company}}
handle {{manual_process}} today? Most owners tell me it's more manual than
they'd like.
```

## Cadence (per contact)
| Day | Channel | Action |
|-----|---------|--------|
| 0 | Email | Email 1 |
| 0 | LinkedIn | Connection note |
| 3 | Email | Email 2 |
| 4 | LinkedIn | Follow-up (if accepted) |
| 7 | Email | Email 3 |
| 12 | Email | Email 4 (breakup) |

Stop the sequence immediately on any reply — hand off to the Sales Agent.
